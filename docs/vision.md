# tenth — Vision

## What is tenth?

tenth is a personal algorithmic trading research and execution platform.

It is designed to discover, test and eventually execute systematic trading strategies using market data, defined rules and disciplined risk management.

The initial objective is not to automate live trading immediately.

tenth will progress through:

Research → Backtesting → Simulation → Paper Trading → Live Trading

Live capital is only introduced once a strategy has demonstrated sufficient evidence of an edge.

---

## Ambition

The original ambition behind tenth is to explore whether systematic trading can generate exceptional compounded returns, with 10% weekly growth used as an aspirational experimental target rather than an assumed or guaranteed return.

Performance must always be considered alongside risk.

tenth should therefore optimise for:

- return
- consistency
- drawdown
- risk-adjusted performance
- capital preservation

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Evidence over instinct

Trading decisions should be supported by data and predefined rules rather than emotion.

### 2. Test before trade

No strategy should trade real money without first being backtested and paper traded.

### 3. Protect capital

Avoiding catastrophic loss takes priority over maximising short-term returns.

### 4. Measure everything

Every signal, trade, result and strategy should be measurable and reviewable.

### 5. Start simple

Simple strategies that can be understood and tested are preferable to unnecessary complexity.

### 6. Separate research from execution

Strategy discovery, testing, risk management and trade execution should remain modular components of tenth.

### 7. Humans retain control

tenth should provide clear visibility of what it is doing and why.

---

## Initial MVP

The first useful version of tenth should be able to:

1. Retrieve historical market data.
2. Define a simple trading strategy.
3. Generate buy and sell signals.
4. Backtest those signals against historical data.
5. Simulate realistic trading costs.
6. Calculate profit and loss.
7. Calculate key performance and risk metrics.
8. Compare the strategy against a benchmark.
9. Produce a readable summary of the results.

No real money is required for the MVP.

Potential later capabilities include:

- strategy comparison
- automated strategy research
- portfolio management
- position sizing
- stop-loss and exit management
- market regime detection
- paper trading
- broker integration
- automated execution
- dashboards and alerts
- AI-assisted research
- continuous performance monitoring

---

## Success

tenth succeeds if it becomes a reliable system for answering one fundamental question:

> Does this trading strategy have sufficient evidence of a repeatable edge to justify risking capital?
